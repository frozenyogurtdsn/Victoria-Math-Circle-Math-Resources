### In-session problem set

1. Find the number of pairs of positive integers $(x, y)$ such that:
   $$xy + 3x + 2y = 2026$$
   *(~AMC 10 level)*

2. A rectangle with integer side lengths $a$ and $b$ has the property that its area is numerically equal to twice its perimeter. Find the sum of all possible values of $a + b$.
   *(~AMC 11 level)*

3. Two high school students, Alex and Beth, are pooling their savings to watch the movie, Niulai. If Alex gives Beth \$12, Beth will have three times as much money as Alex. If Beth instead gives Alex \$12, Alex will have $k$ times as much money as Beth, where $k$ is a positive integer. Find the maximum possible amount of money Beth could have initially.
   *(~Cayley level)*

4. Find the number of ordered pairs of positive integers $(x, y)$ that satisfy the equation:
   $$\frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{y} = \frac{1}{12} \quad \text{such that } x < y$$
   *(~AIME level)*

5. A right triangle has legs of integer lengths $a$ and $b$, and hypotenuse of length $c$. If the area of the triangle is equal to its perimeter, find all possible ordered pairs $(a, b)$ with $a \le b$.
   *(~AIME level)*

6. Determine all pairs of positive integers $(m, n)$ such that $mn - 2m - 3n$ is a prime number $p$, and $m + n = 16$.
   *(~COMC level)*

7. Find all triples of positive integers $(x, y, z)$ such that:
   $$xy + yz + zx + x + y + z = 2026 \quad \text{such that } x \le y \le z$$
   *(~COMC level)*

### Take-Home challenge set

8. Find all ordered triples of positive integers $(a, b, c)$ with $a < b < c$ that satisfy:
   $$\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{c} = 1$$
   *Show how this can be solved using nested SFFT or logical bounding, and write down your full proof.*

9. Find all pairs of integers $(x, y)$ that satisfy the equation:
   $$x^2y - 3x^2 - 4y + 11 = 0$$
   *(Hint: Group terms to isolate $y$ or transform it into a polynomials-based SFFT structure.)*
