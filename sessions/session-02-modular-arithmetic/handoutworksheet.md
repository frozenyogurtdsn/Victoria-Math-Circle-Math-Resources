# Victoria Math Circle - Session 2
## Modular Arithmetic & Divisibility Rules

**Topic:** Congruence Properties, Fermat's Little Theorem & Advanced Divisibility

**Core Identity:**
* If $a \equiv b \pmod{m}$, then $a^k \equiv b^k \pmod{m}$.
* **Fermat's Little Theorem (FLT):** $a^{p-1} \equiv 1 \pmod{p}$ for prime $p$.

---

### In-Session Problem Set

1. **Find the remainder when $7^{2026}$ is divided by 19.**  
   *(~AMC 10 level)*

2. **Let $N$ be the 2026-digit positive integer formed by repeating the digits '13' consecutively:**  
   $$N = 131313\dots1313$$  
   Determine whether $N$ is divisible by 99, and find the remainder when $N$ is divided by 11.  
   *(~AMC 12 level)*

3. **Find the smallest positive integer $n$ such that $n$ is a multiple of 5, and satisfies the congruence:**  
   $$2n \equiv n \pmod{7}$$  
   *(~AIME level)*

---

### Take-Home Challenge Set

4. **Prove that $n^{13} - n$ is divisible by 2730 for all positive integers $n$.**  
   *(Hint: Prime factorize 2730, and apply Fermat's Little Theorem to each prime factor separately.)*  
   *(~COMC level)*

5. **Find the number of integers $n$ with $1 \le n \le 1000$ such that $3^n + 1$ is divisible by 10.**  
   *(~AIME level)*
