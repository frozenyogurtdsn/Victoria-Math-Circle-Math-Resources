## 🌟 1. Core Learning Platforms

* **Art of Problem Solving (AoPS)**
  * **Alcumus**: A free, adaptive online practice system designed to help students master core topics across Algebra, Geometry, Number Theory, and Counting & Probability.
  * **AoPS Wiki**: Contains an extensive database of past AMC 8/10/12, AIME, and other math contest problems with detailed solutions.
* **Khan Academy**
  * Great for solidifying high school mathematics foundations (Pre-Calculus, Algebra, and Geometry).

## 🏆 2. Past Contests & Official Practice Material

### Canadian Mathematics Contests (CMS & Waterloo CEMC)
* **CEMC Waterloo Past Contests**
  * **Link**: [CEMC Official Past Contests](https://www.cemc.uwaterloo.ca/contests/past_contests.html)
  * **Recommended**: Pascal, Cayley, Fermat (Grades 9–11) and the **Euclid Contest** (Senior Flagship Competition). Official solutions are provided for all past papers.
* **COMC (Canadian Open Mathematics Challenge)**
  * The premier national competition in Canada. Excellent for students looking to tackle written proof-based and multi-step logic problems.

### North American Standard Contests (MAA / AMC)
* **AMC 10 / AMC 12 (American Mathematics Competitions)**
  * The most widely recognized multiple-choice contest series in North America. Full contest archives from 2000 to the present are available on the AoPS Wiki.

## 💡 4. VMC Recommended Learning Roadmap

1. **Foundational Phase**: Master the high school core curriculum while building topic-specific proficiency on AoPS Alcumus.
2. **Technique Acquisition**: Learn essential competition techniques (e.g., Simon's Favorite Factoring Trick, Pigeonhole Principle, Modular Arithmetic basics).
3. **Contest Simulation**: 1 to 2 months prior to a target contest, practice recent 5-year past papers under timed conditions and thoroughly review official solutions.
